Making financial services clear and credible

Client
Luzeel Taxation & Financial Services LLC
Role
Advertising Copywriter
Dates
May 2023 – March 2025

The challenge

Tax and financial services are dense with jargon, fees and disclaimers, and that complexity creates hesitation at the moment of decision. The objective was to communicate services in plain, confident language while keeping all consumer-facing copy within regulatory standards.

Approach

  • Distilled technical tax and financial concepts into clear, persuasive copy for landing pages and email campaigns.
  • Partnered with legal and compliance teams to ensure messaging met financial regulatory standards.
  • Developed a messaging framework that kept the brand voice consistent across digital channels.

Sample rewrite

Original writing sample

Before: “Pursuant to regulatory disclosure standards, select your primary income categorization to initiate preliminary tax liability assessment.”

After: “Let’s estimate your taxes. Choose your main source of income to get started.”

Sample email opening

Original writing sample

Subject: Your tax estimate, explained in plain English

Filing season doesn’t have to feel like a pop quiz. In three short steps, we’ll show you where you stand, why, and what to do next. No jargon, no surprises.
